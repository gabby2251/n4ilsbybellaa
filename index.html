Booking Policy

💳

Payment

$10 deposit required at booking via Cash App or Apple Pay. Remaining balance can be paid in cash, Apple Pay, or Cash App.

👥

Guests

No extra guests are allowed. Please reschedule if you are feeling sick.

⏰

Late Policy

You have a 10-minute grace period. After that, a $15 fee will be applied. After 15 minutes, ur appointment will be cancelled and a new deposit will be required.

❌

Cancellations

Cancellations and reschedules must be made 24 hrs in advance. I allow 2 reschedules with the same deposit. After that, a new deposit will be required. Same-day cancellations forfeit the deposit.

Important

Before Your Appointment

01

Bring Inspiration

If you have any nail style in mind, bring photos or references. Feel free to DM me on Instagram regarding any questions on a nail set.

02

Arrive Bare-Handed

Arrive bare-handed to ur appointment unless you booked for a soak off. I do not work over other people's work or damaged nails.

03

Send Your Deposit

Make sure you are sending ur deposit as soon as you book. Failure to send deposit means ur appointment will be canceled.

04

Arrive On Time

Arrive to ur appointment on time to ensure no rushing. You have a 10-minute grace period — after that, a $15 fee applies.
